# Glossary

## 4.1 Glossary

- **Order**: one request from an outlet for one delivery date; one brand; `chilled` or `ambient`; has lines; has weight and volume. Allocation key.
- **Stop**: one order on a trip (one order = one stop).
- **Trip**: orders delivered by one vehicle in one outing to **one district, one brand**. A vehicle runs at most 2 trips a day (trip_no 1 or 2).
- **Run**: everything a vehicle does in a day (its trips).
- **Planning day**: a depot + delivery date; owns the draft, published plan versions and its state.
- **Plan version**: immutable numbered publication of a planning day's plan.
- **Deferral**: an order not served on its requested date, with a recorded reason.
- **Fresh class** vs **Style/Tech class**: two time-budget classes (section 4.3).
